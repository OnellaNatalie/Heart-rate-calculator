The Physiology Behind Fat Oxidation

At 55% to 70% of Heart Rate Reserve (HRR), oxygen availability in muscle mitochondria is optimal for Beta-Oxidation (converting body fat into usable energy).

If heart rate exceeds 75% BPM, the body shifts rapidly to Anaerobic Glycolysis, burning carbohydrates/glucose instead of fat!
